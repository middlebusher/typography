Artifact roles

Canvas and surface

canvas, surface, and raised replace raw palette references so the same artifact can move between cream and ink.

Editorial typography

Display 64/68, title 48/54, section 32/40, heading 24/32, body 20/28 and 16/24. Hack is reserved for labels and metrics.

Contrast-bearing accents

Cream and white use darker primary, secondary, and tertiary step 11 foregrounds. Ink surfaces use their lighter dark-theme counterparts; step 9 remains the fill.

Presentation spacing

An 8–48 px role scale supports page margins, editorial rhythm, card padding, grids, and fixed-canvas composition.

Product evidence

Product screenshots and UI previews retain Carrot UI semantics inside a marketing composition; the surrounding story uses marketing roles.

Format profiles

Deck, one-pager, document, web campaign, and print profiles inherit the same tokens but set their own canvas size and density.

Governance
01
Carrot UI owns product behavior.

Do not copy marketing values back into application components or interaction states.

02
Marketing owns editorial expression.

Use semantic artifact roles instead of hard-coding cream, ink, or accent scale steps.

03
Color-family names stay literal.

Use orange, mint, and periwinkle in foundations; primary, secondary, and tertiary belong in the semantic layer.

04
Theme parity is required.

Every canonical template must render and pass contrast review in both cream-light and ink-dark modes.
